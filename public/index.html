Settings / Safety

The correct user-facing term for people connected to the app is CONNECTED PARTICIPANTS (or PEOPLE ONLINE), not “connected brains”.

This build uses the NEURO V8 Worker directly: neuro-v8.univonra.workers.dev. It does not require the old satellite relay Worker. Internet/mobile WebSocket transport carries digital messages and events. Actual brain-signal detection requires compatible EEG/BCI hardware.
